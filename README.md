# HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze employee attrition and workforce trends. The dashboard enables HR professionals to identify the major factors contributing to employee turnover and supports data-driven decision-making to improve employee retention, workforce planning, and organizational performance.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyze employee attrition using data-driven insights and help the organization:

- Improve employee retention
- Reduce employee attrition
- Identify high-risk employee groups
- Support strategic HR decision-making
- Enhance overall workforce performance

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides an interactive analysis of employee attrition through various KPIs, charts, and filters.

### Department Filter (Slicer)

- Human Resources
- Research & Development
- Sales

The slicer allows users to filter the entire dashboard by department for detailed analysis.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- 👥 Total Employees
- 🚪 Total Attrition
- 📉 Attrition Rate
- 💰 Average Salary
- 🎂 Average Age
- 📅 Average Years at Company

### Dashboard Visualizations

- Attrition by Education Field (Doughnut Chart)
- Attrition by Age Group (Clustered Column Chart)
- Attrition by Gender (Cards)
- Attrition by Salary Slab (Horizontal Bar Chart)
- Attrition by Years at Company (Line Chart)
- Attrition by Job Role (Horizontal Bar Chart)
- Job Role-wise Attrition (Matrix/Table)

## 📈 Key Business Insights

- The organization has **1,470 employees**, out of which **237 employees have left**, resulting in an **attrition rate of 16.1%**.

- Employees aged **26–35 years** have the highest attrition, indicating that early-career professionals are more likely to switch jobs.

- Employees from **Life Sciences** and **Medical** education backgrounds contribute the largest share of employee attrition.

- Employees earning **up to 5K salary** experience the highest attrition, suggesting compensation plays a significant role in employee turnover.

- Employee attrition is highest during the **initial years of employment**, highlighting the need for improved onboarding, mentoring, and employee engagement.

- **Sales Executives, Laboratory Technicians, and Research Scientists** represent the largest workforce segments and require continuous monitoring due to their significant contribution to overall attrition.

- The workforce consists of more **male employees than female employees**, making gender distribution an important factor while analyzing attrition patterns.
